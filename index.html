About

I'm Rya, a game designer who likes small, warm games: the kind you can play in a lunch break and still think about later. I care about tiny details, the crossword on a passenger's lap, the steam off a pot, the way a crowd leans in on a long rally.

Every game here runs in the browser with no installs, so you can click a card and play.
