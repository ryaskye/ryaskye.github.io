About

I'm Rya, a software engineer and game designer. I like owning a project from the first idea to the version people actually use, whether that's an app on the App Store or a game that runs in a browser tab.

On the engineering side I build complete, self-contained projects: state machines that drive branching stories, physics and AI for sports sims, real-time 3D rendering and shaders, and sound made entirely in code. On the design side I care about the small details that make something feel good to use, like the crossword on a passenger's lap or the steam off a pot.
